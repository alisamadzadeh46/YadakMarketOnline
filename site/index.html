YadakMarket Online

A multi-vendor marketplace for automotive spare parts. Suppliers sell to retail customers and to wholesale shops through one storefront, and every sale is split automatically between the supplier and the marketplace with a floating commission.

View the source See the screenshots

Highlights

Built with Django REST Framework and Next.js, running in Docker. The interface is Persian (right-to-left, Jalali calendar).

Multi-vendor

Each supplier manages its own products, orders, coupons, reports and earnings. The site owner controls the whole marketplace.

Floating commission

A default rate that moves inside a min/max band, separate rates for shops and retail customers, and per-category overrides.

Automatic revenue split

Every confirmed order records the marketplace commission and each supplier's net share. VAT, shipping and supplier coupons are handled correctly.

Wholesale ready

Tiered volume pricing, minimum order quantities, carton sizes, KYC-approved shops and credit accounts with SMS reminders.

Three ways to pay

Card-to-card with receipt upload and a countdown, online payment (ZarinPal or BitPay), and credit purchases.

Blog and SEO

Rich-text blog with a Yoast-style analyzer, sitemap, structured data, and feeds for price-comparison sites.

How the commission is split

For each order line the most specific rate wins: category, then buyer type, then the default. Commission is charged on goods only, never on VAT or shipping.

Panels for every role

Buyers

  • Filters, car finder, search suggestions
  • Server-side cart and address book with map
  • Order history, invoices, reorder

Suppliers

  • Dashboard with low-stock forecast
  • Order workflow with new-order alerts
  • Sales report with CSV export, earnings

Site owner

  • KYC review and credit accounts
  • Categories, blog, slider, SMS log
  • Gateway, payment methods, VAT and shipping

Revenue-share partner

  • Commission ledger with CSV export
  • Rate settings within the agreed band
